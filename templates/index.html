Chronological Progression
2019-02-01 SCHEME LAUNCH

Interim Union Budget Announcement

Interim Finance Minister Piyush Goyal announced the scheme with an annual allocation of ₹75,000 crore, effective retrospectively from December 1, 2018.

Ministry of Finance / Union Cabinet
PIB Release Archive
2019-02-24 NATIONWIDE ROLLOUT

Formal Inauguration by Prime Minister Narendra Modi

Formally launched at Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, disbursing the first ₹2,000 electronic installment to over 10 million registered farmers via DBT.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi
PIB PRID 1566139
2019-05-31 ELIGIBILITY EXPANSION

Removal of 2-Hectare Landholding Limit

The Union Cabinet in its first meeting of the 2nd NDA term expanded the scheme to cover all farmer families regardless of the size of their cultivable landholding, adding approximately 20 million additional beneficiaries.

Union Cabinet Resolution
Cabinet Notification
2022-05-21 POLICY AMENDMENT

Mandatory Aadhaar e-KYC and Land Seeding

To prevent leakages and weed out ineligible recipients, Ministry of Agriculture made OTP-based or biometric e-KYC, physical land record verification, and Aadhaar-NPCI bank seeding compulsory for receiving future installments.
